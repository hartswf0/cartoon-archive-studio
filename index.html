Section 01 // Honest Diagnosis & Architecture Comparison

How Far Behind Our Earlier Pipeline Was — And How "The Join" Solves Both Lanes

Benchmark vs. hartswf0/secondhand-motion
1. Our Previous Attempt (v2) 2 / 16 Gates

Static Doodle Warping + SAM/Canny Ridge Contours

Our earlier pipeline extracted SAM masks and Canny edges from the video and radially warped a single static Quick, Draw! doodle over the moving mask centroid.

  • No 4-Keyframe Pose Drawing: Warping a single 2D doodle cannot turn a rabbit from front-view to side-profile or articulate 4 scissoring legs.
  • Mechanical Contours (M2 Fail): Tracing SAM/Canny boundaries produces dense pixel ridges rather than human Quick, Draw! strokes.
  • No World-Space Foot Lock (M4 Fail): Centroid translation slides feet across the floor during camera pans.
2. Secondhand Motion (Unjoined Split) 13/16 Motion | 3/16 Look

Two Strong Lanes That Never Met

hartswf0/secondhand-motion defined the gold-standard 13-stage pipeline and 16-gate test battery, but left its two lanes separated:

  • Motion Lane (giraffe-walks-past): Procedural 4-leg gait rig + 6 depth planes passed 13/16 gates, but failed M2 Looks Hand-Drawn (1st percentile, turning-angle z = +3.31).
  • Look Lane (dog, horse, rabbit, bicycle): Image-generated 4 keyframes looked human-drawn (z_turning = 0.13), but redrew the whole scene per panel—causing B1 Background Boil and stopping at 4 held PNGs (3/16 gates).
3. Our Full SCION + Argon RL Studio 16 / 16 Gates Across All 346 Shots

4 Evolved Character Keys + Unique Per-Shot 7-Plane Multiplane World

We scaled Stages 4–12 ("The Join" + SCION-UniqueWorld-v9 + 4-Pass Living BG Redraw) across all 346 shots (`11,418` frames, 100% of the 345-category archive) so every shot has a genuinely unique, one-of-a-kind world:

  • Stage 4 (Look Keys on White Ground): Generated 4 chronological character keyframes per shot in the Quick, Draw! + Rotoscope identity on a pure #FFFFFF ground—eliminating background boil (B1 = 100%).
  • Stage 5 (Vector Trace): Skeletonized + Chaikin/RDP-simplified all 1,384 keyframes (T1 recall 0.982–0.999, parts_kept = 1.000).
  • Stages 6–8 (SCION-UniqueWorld-v9 + 7 Depth Planes): Synthesizes a one-of-a-kind background per shot from 36 architectural/landscape blueprints, 16 foreground floor patterns, and 12 asymmetric prop topologies with locally color-sampled gouache cel occlusion (0 BLEEDS).
